# Marketing, Sales & Cold Outreach Playbook
## High-Ticket Customer Acquisition for Mobile Stage Rentals

---

## 1. High-Value Target Personas & Value Hooks

### Persona 1: Municipalities & Parks / Special Events Directors
* **Who They Are:** City Event Coordinators, Parks & Recreation Directors, Downtown Development Authorities (DDAs).
* **Their Pain Point:** Setting up modular scaffolding stages takes 4–6 crew members, several hours, permits, and leaves grounds damaged.
* **Our Core Value Hook:** *"Zero-footprint, 20-minute setup mobile stage that saves city staff 20+ man-hours per event while providing full weather coverage with a 21' power awning."*

### Persona 2: Race Directors & Sports Event Coordinators (5K / 10K / Marathons / Triathlons)
* **Who They Are:** Endurance event companies, charity run directors, cycling race organizers.
* **Their Pain Point:** Need an instant awards ceremony & starting/finish line stage at 5:00 AM on closed city streets, then must clear the street immediately after the race.
* **Our Core Value Hook:** *"Drive-in, deploy in 15 mins for your starting announcements, DJ, and awards ceremony, and roll out 30 minutes after the finish line without blocking traffic."*

### Persona 3: Experiential Marketing & PR Agencies
* **Who They Are:** Creative directors, brand activation managers, event production agencies (working for beverage, tech, lifestyle brands).
* **Their Pain Point:** Custom pop-up builds cost $50k+ and take weeks to construct.
* **Our Core Value Hook:** *"Fully wrappable commercial mobile marketing stage with interior A/C green room/lounge, on-board 50A power, and high-foot-traffic activation capabilities."*

### Persona 4: Music Festival & Concert Promoters (Secondary Stages)
* **Who They Are:** Regional festival directors, fair coordinators, winery/brewery series promoters.
* **Their Pain Point:** High cost of renting multiple full-truss stages for secondary acoustic/DJ stages.
* **Our Core Value Hook:** *"Plug-and-play acoustic / secondary stage with integrated sound & lighting, delivered turnkey at a fraction of modular rigging cost."*

## 4. Inbound Lead Generation Engine (High Intent Channels)

1. **Google Local Search Ads (High Intent, Immediate Bookings):**
   * *Keywords:* "mobile stage rental [city]", "stage rental for festival", "portable concert stage rental near me", "event stage hire".
   * *Landing Page Goal:* Instant dimension & price estimate calculator with date picker.
2. **Local Event Vendor Directories & Chamber of Commerce:**
   * Join your local City Chamber of Commerce and Regional Tourism / Visitors Bureau (e.g., Visit Nashville / Music City Convention Center vendor list).
3. **AV Production Company Referral Partnerships:**
   * Local sound & lighting companies often get asked for stages but don't own a mobile stage trailer. Offer them a **15% finder's fee / wholesale partner discount** to cross-sell your stage into their existing sound jobs.
4. **Permanent Rig Magnet / QR Code Signage:**
   * When the trailer is deployed at high-visibility public events, have a sleek branded banner on the stage skirt or corner uprights: *"Stage provided by [Company Name] - Book for your next event: [Website/QR Code]"*.
