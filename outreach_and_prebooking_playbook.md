## 1. Strategy Overview: The 50% Deposit Advance Booking Engine

In the event production industry, locking in dates 2 to 6 months in advance is standard operating procedure. Event directors routinely pay a **50% non-refundable deposit upon contract signing** to guarantee equipment and date exclusivity.

### Target Numbers:
* **Target Bookings**: 4 advance event reservations
* **Average Booking Value**: $2,750 / day
* **50% Deposit per Event**: $1,375.00
* **Upfront Cash Collected Before Delivery**: **$5,500.00**
* *Use of Funds*: Directly covers dealer loan down-payment, commercial insurance binder, and trailer registration fees.

## 4. The "Risk-Free" Pre-Booking Deposit Closer

When an event organizer says they want to hold a date, use this **Risk-Free Deposit Agreement**:

1. **Send Date Hold Agreement**:
   * *“To lock in your date exclusively on our master production calendar, we collect a standard 50% deposit ($1,375). The remaining 50% is due the morning of the event.”*
2. **The "Guaranteed Delivery" Clause**:
   * *“Your deposit guarantees our rig, sound engineer, and stage platform exclusively for your date. In the rare event of severe weather rescheduling, your deposit transfers 100% to any mutually agreed makeup date within 12 months.”*
3. **Payment Collection**:
   * Collect via ACH transfer, business check, or credit card invoicing (via Stripe / QuickBooks).
