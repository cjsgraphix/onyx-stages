[04 // LOGISTICS & VENUE REQUIREMENTS]

Site Footprint & Technical Guidelines

Footprint Needed

Minimum 35' length × 18' width clear footprint. Level concrete, asphalt, hard-packed gravel, or firm turf ground required.

Setup & Teardown

Arrives 2 hours prior to doors. Stage deployment, rail assembly, and leveling completed in 20–30 minutes by our delivery specialist.

Shore Power Hookup

50-Amp NEMA 14-50 standard plug or 100A panel hookup. When off-grid, our silent battery or inverter generator powers all loads.

Structural & Safety Standards

Commercial-grade structural steel chassis, dual heavy-duty corner stabilizing jacks, safety perimeter handrails, and slip-resistant stage surface.
