## 2. Rental Package & Pricing Matrix

### Tier 1: "The Dry Stage" (Self-Serve / Production-Ready)
*Target Market: Experienced event planners, touring bands, AV companies with their own gear.*
* **What’s Included:**
  * 24' ATC Stage Trailer with 16' fold-out stage, handrails, aluminum entry steps, and 21' power awning with integrated lighting.
  * Shore power 50A hookup cable (50ft).
  * Delivery, leveling, stage deployment, and post-event teardown (within 30 miles).
* **Pricing:**
  * **Single Day (Up to 8 hrs on site):** \$1,450
  * **2-Day Weekend (Sat–Sun):** \$2,400
  * **3-Day Festival (Fri–Sun):** \$3,200
  * **Weekly Rate:** \$5,500

### Tier 2: "The Turnkey Live Event" (Most Popular)
*Target Market: Municipal festivals, 5K/marathons, corporate celebrations, outdoor brewery concerts.*
* **What’s Included:**
  * Everything in Tier 1.
  * On-board 50A super-quiet inverter generator with 1 full tank of fuel.
  * High-output PA Sound System (2x QSC K12.2 tops, 1x QSC KS118 sub, digital mixer, iPad control).
  * 2x Shure digital wireless handheld mics + 1x wireless lapel mic.
  * 4x High-output LED RGBW stage wash lights.
  * On-site AV technician for setup and sound check.
* **Pricing:**
  * **Single Day:** \$2,750
  * **2-Day Weekend:** \$4,600
  * **3-Day Festival:** \$5,950

### Tier 3: "Brand Activation / Pop-Up Tour" (High-Margin B2B)
*Target Market: Marketing agencies, beverage brands, corporate product launches, experiential roadshows.*
* **What’s Included:**
  * Clean white stage trailer ready for custom magnetic or vinyl branding.
  * Climate-controlled interior lounge (A/C & heat) with interior LED lighting & 65" 4K presentation display.
  * On-board continuous power generator.
  * Interior prep/green-room area with coin TPO flooring and presentation bar.
  * Dedicated logistics driver & stage manager on-call.
* **Pricing:**
  * **Weekend Activation (2 Days):** \$5,500 + Wrap Production Fee
  * **1-Week Tour Stop:** \$9,500
  * **30-Day Multi-City Tour:** \$24,000 – \$32,000 / month (plus mileage/per diem)

## 6. Key Financial Insights & Risk Mitigation
1. **Asset Value Preservation:** Unlike motorhomes or food trucks with complex engine repairs, all-aluminum ATC trailers have almost zero frame rust and retain 70–80% of their resale value even after 5 years.
2. **Break-Even Point:** At **\$1,195/month** in overhead, you only need **one 1-day dry rental per month** to cover all base carrying costs. Everything after booking #1 each month is pure profit.
3. **Multi-Unit Scaling:** Once the first trailer reaches 70% weekend utilization during peak season (April–October), financing a second unit out of cash flow produces compounding EBITDA without additional marketing overhead.
