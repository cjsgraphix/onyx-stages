## 3. Tech Stack Recommendation for Rapid Launch

1. **Standalone High-Speed Web App:** Pure HTML5/TailwindCSS/JS with zero subscription fees, lightning fast load speeds (<1 sec), and mobile-responsive quote calculation.
2. **Booking & CRM Integration:** Embed HoneyBook, Booqable, or Formspree directly for automated deposit collection via Stripe and calendar sync.
3. **Analytics:** Google Analytics 4 + Google Ads conversion tracking for local searches ("stage rental near me").
